Sunday = Revision Only

1, 8, 15, 22 and 29 November are revision-only Sundays. No new chapter is scheduled on these days.

Sunday 1 · 1 Nov

Diagnostic revision: revise previously studied basics in all six subjects; organise notebooks and identify weak topics.

Sunday 2 · 8 Nov

Revise study days 1–6: Maths, Science, SST, English, Hindi A and IT. Redo incorrect questions.

Sunday 3 · 15 Nov

Revise study days 7–12. Practise formulas, reactions, maps, literature extracts and IT definitions.

Sunday 4 · 22 Nov

Revise study days 13–18. Practise proofs, diagrams, long answers, grammar and practical steps.

Sunday 5 · 29 Nov

Revise study days 19–24. Mixed subject tests, map work, error notebook and weak areas.

Monday · 30 Nov

Final study day: Day 25 mixed tests and final corrections in all six subjects.

Sleep reminder: The requested 12 AM–4 AM timing allows only 4 hours of sleep. Teenagers generally need 8–10 hours. This planner preserves your requested timetable slots, but 4 hours is not a healthy sleep routine; please make room for adequate sleep with a parent/guardian.
